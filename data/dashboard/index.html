How to read your utility meter
Find the kWh total

Most digital meters cycle between the accumulated kWh total and instantaneous power. Wait for the kWh screen.

Write down every digit

Enter the running total exactly as shown, including leading zeros and any decimal part.

Match the unit

Confirm the meter labels the value as kWh before saving the baseline reading.

Recheck later

A second reading a day later lets you compare meter-reported usage against Wattrack's pulse count.

The baseline is kept locally in this browser and posted to the ESP32 once a firmware endpoint for it exists.

info How Your Submeter Calculates Energy

Each blink of the meter's LED or pulse output = 1 impulse. At 1,000 imp/kWh, each blink precisely equals 1.00 Wh (0.001 kWh).

Energy per pulse: 1.000 Wh

Welcome to Wattrack

Wattrack counts the pulse signals from your non-invasive submeter and turns them into live kWh and cost estimates — all processed locally on the ESP32.

  • Live power, usage and month-end projections
  • Pulse constant & utility rate saved to ESP32 flash
  • No cloud account — everything stays on your LAN
